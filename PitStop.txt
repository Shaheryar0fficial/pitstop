# PitStop — MVP Feature Document
### Community Platform for Pakistani Heavy Bike Owners

---

## Problem Statement

Pakistani heavy bike owners have no trusted space to find skilled mechanics, buy genuine parts, or get real advice — they're stuck between overpriced shops and chaotic Facebook groups.

---

## The Core Idea

A user posts a **need** — service, part, or question.
The platform **intelligently routes** that need to the right people.
The right people **respond, connect, and transact**.

> You need detailing → only detailers see it.
> You need headlights → all parts sellers get notified.
> You have a weird engine noise → community diagnoses it.

---

## User Types

| Type | Who They Are |
|---|---|
| **Rider** | Heavy bike owner posting needs, buying parts, asking questions |
| **Service Provider** | Mechanic, detailer, tuner, modifier — lists their skill |
| **Parts Seller** | Individual or shop selling genuine/used parts |

---

## Feature 1 — Smart Need Posting (Core Loop)

Rider posts a need in natural language.

**AI Layer:** Classifies the post into one of three categories:
- `SERVICE_REQUEST` → routes to relevant service providers only
- `PARTS_REQUEST` → notifies all parts sellers
- `COMMUNITY_QUESTION` → goes to public feed for crowd advice

**Post fields:**
- Bike model + year
- Need description (free text)
- Photos (optional)
- Location (city-level)

**How routing works:**
- Service providers tag their skills on signup (detailing, tuning, engine work, electrical, modification, tyres etc.)
- A detailing request only pings detailers. A tuning request only pings tuners.
- Parts requests broadcast to everyone selling parts.

---

## Feature 2 — Service Provider Profiles

Mechanics and service providers get a public profile:

- Name, city, skills tags
- Bike brands/models they work on
- Portfolio photos
- Ratings + reviews from past riders
- Contact / booking button

**AI Layer:** When a service request comes in, AI ranks nearby providers by skill match + rating before showing results.

---

## Feature 3 — Parts Marketplace

Peer-to-peer parts listing:

- Part name, bike compatibility, condition (new/used), price
- Seller profile + trust score
- Photos required
- WhatsApp/in-app contact

**AI Layer:** When a rider posts a parts need, AI matches it against active listings and surfaces exact or compatible matches first.

---

## Feature 4 — Community Feed

Open feed for questions, ride posts, and advice:

- Anyone can post
- Replies from verified riders and providers
- Upvote system — best answer rises
- Tagged by bike model so owners of same bike find relevant threads

**AI Layer:** AI suggests relevant past threads before you post ("Someone already asked this — here's what riders said")

---

## Feature 5 — AI Bike Assistant (The Differentiator)

Embedded chatbot trained on common heavy bike issues.

- Describe your problem → AI gives probable causes + what type of mechanic you need
- Helps rider understand if it's a DIY fix or needs a pro
- Prevents riders from getting ripped off by knowing what the job should cost roughly

**Example:**
> "My CBR 150 is vibrating at high speed"
> AI: "Likely wheel balancing issue or loose front axle nut. This is a 30-min job. Book a general mechanic, should cost Rs. 500-800."

## Tech Stack (Simple, MVP-ready)

| Layer | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Frontend | Next.js | Fast, SEO-friendly, one codebase |
| Backend | Node.js + Express | Simple REST APIs |
| Database | MongoDB | Flexible schema for posts/profiles |
| AI Classification | Claude API | Classify posts, rank matches, chatbot |
| Auth | Clerk or NextAuth | Fast to plug in |
| File Uploads | Cloudinary | Free tier, easy image handling |
| Deployment | Vercel + Railway | Free, fast |
