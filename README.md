# PitStop

A community platform for Pakistani heavy bike owners to find the right mechanic, buy genuine parts, and get real advice.

## Categories (locked)

The rider picks one of three sections. AI works inside each section; it does not choose the section.

1. **Service Providers** — mechanics, detailers, tuners, modifiers. AI works out the exact skill the request needs and ranks providers by skill match, city and rating.
2. **Parts & Stores** — shops and individual sellers. AI matches requests to listings, showing exact or compatible parts first.
3. **Forums** — community questions and advice. AI shows similar past threads before you post.

Plus an **AI Bike Assistant** on every page: it diagnoses problems, suggests DIY vs. pro, estimates cost, and points the rider to the right section.
